Loveroom near Lyon:
how to choose the right one

The word «loveroom» covers the best and the worst: from a studio with an inflatable spa to a suite designed down to the last detail. Here's how to tell the difference before you pay.

Demand around Lyon has exploded, and supply has followed — not always with the same standards. The same listing title can mean a hotel room with a corner bathtub, or a place built entirely for two. These six criteria let you tell them apart in a few minutes.

1. Is it an entire property, or just a room?

This is the criterion that changes everything. In a room, you share a corridor, an entrance, sometimes a wall with neighbours. In an entire property, you close the door and no one else exists.

Look for the words "entire place" and check that no space is shared. Silence is the first luxury of a stay for two.

2. Is the jacuzzi actually inside the property?

Three set-ups circulate under the same name, and the gap in comfort is considerable:

  • Shared spa — common to the whole establishment, swimwear required, set hours
  • Bookable spa — yours, but on a reserved one- or two-hour slot
  • Private spa — inside your own property, accessible at all times

Only the third option lets you bathe at midnight or straight out of bed without a second thought.

3. What's the bedding actually like?

This is the most overlooked point about loverooms, and the most decisive one. A beautiful spa never makes up for a bad night's sleep. Look for mentions of hotel-grade bedding and a mattress topper, and above all read the reviews: guests mention it spontaneously when it's good.

4. Is check-in self-service?

Arriving at a front desk, queuing, crossing paths with other guests: all of it keeps you in the world you were trying to leave. Self check-in via a smart lock changes the start of the stay completely — a code, a door, and you're already somewhere else.

5. How many guests does the place accept?

A property built for four and rented to two doesn't offer the same intimacy as a place designed for a single couple. Maximum capacity says a lot about intent: a strict two-guest limit means every square metre was designed for the two of you.

6. Do the photos match reality?

The best indicator isn't how good the photos look, but what guests say about them. Look for reviews that mention "exactly like the photos" — a far more reliable signal than a wide-angle shot.

How much does a loveroom near Lyon cost?

Rates vary a great deal with the season and the day of the week. Expect to pay €120 to €300 a night for an entire property with a private spa in the Lyon area, with peaks on Saturdays and over the holidays.

One tip that saves money: weeknights are often noticeably cheaper, and the place is exactly the same. If your schedules allow it, a Tuesday beats a Saturday — for the price as much as for the quiet.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between a loveroom and a hotel room with a jacuzzi?

A loveroom is an entire property, privatised for a single couple: no reception, no shared space, and a spa accessible at any time. A hotel room with a jacuzzi remains part of a shared establishment, with neighbours and often set access times for the spa.
