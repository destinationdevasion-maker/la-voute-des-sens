01 The place

Leave the world at the door.

On a quiet street, a white door with no sign. Nothing on the façade suggests what lies beneath it.

Below, an old stone vault has been stripped back to its bones and rebuilt around a single idea — two people, and nothing else to think about.

No reception. No corridor. No neighbours. You arrive on your own, and time stops behaving normally.

iDisconnect — no schedule, no interruption
iiSlow living — the day is yours to waste
iiiLuxury without excess — nothing shouts here

“Everything has been considered here, so that nothing has to be considered by you.”
